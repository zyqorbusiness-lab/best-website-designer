# award-site-teardown-method

Study award-winning websites and extract techniques, not content: choosing references, the teardown card, a measuring script, DevTools recipes, timing motion by frames, the jury lens, take / never take, and turning a teardown into a chapter of your build.

- `SKILL.md`: the method.
- `scripts/teardown.mjs`: `node scripts/teardown.mjs <url> --out work/teardowns` writes TEARDOWN.md + teardown.json + screenshots (desktop and phone). Needs Playwright.
- `examples/lusion-co.md`, `examples/igloo-inc.md`: finished cards from real runs (30 Oct 2026).
- `examples/index.html`: teardown board against Zyqor's tier budgets.

MIT. Sources in `CREDITS.md`. Studied sites are inspiration only; nothing of theirs is included.
