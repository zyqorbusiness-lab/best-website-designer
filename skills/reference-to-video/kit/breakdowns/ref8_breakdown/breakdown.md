## What to copy (summary)

- Structure: talking head (0-5 s) -> vertical PUSH to a clean white set with a phone mockup (5-16 s) -> hard cut to a full-body cut-out leaning on a giant phone (16.6-24 s) -> spin-blur back to the talking head (24-31.8 s) -> white end card (31.8-33 s). Only 7 real cuts; most scene changes are moves (push, zoom through the phone, spin-blur), so it feels fast without feeling choppy.
- Something changes every 0.4-0.8 s: 32 animation segments in 33 s, the longest still moment is ~0.9 s (15 s). Cards and stickers pop with overshoot (back-out) and leave with ease-in; the spin-blur at 23.6-24.4 s is ease-in into the cut and ease-out after it.
- Text system: inside-the-app captions are bold sans with ONE keyword in lime-yellow italic/bold ('editor', 'problem'); overlay titles are heavy rounded caps ('SOLVE', 'CAPTIONS') with a small light line under them (size ratio ~4:1) and a scramble/pixel reveal.
- Colour: warm orange room light + clean white sets; accents lime-yellow, red, UI blue. The blue glowing app orb (3.6-5 s), cyan RGB-split (10.3 s) and the purple glow behind the IG card (27.5-28.4 s) are exactly the 'AI' look Sujon banned: copy the moves, not those colours.
- Sound (from the audio onsets + what is on screen): swoosh on every card/push, pop on every sticker, UI click on every screen jump/tap, glitch burst on the 3.5/10.3/25.5 s glitches, riser into the 26.6 s light-leak, impact on the 27.2 s return. Voice sits on top the whole time.
- B-roll is cheap and reused: the same app screen recording plays twice (7-10 s and 12-15 s) and nobody notices because the phone is zoomed/positioned differently - re-use b-roll with a new scale/position instead of hunting for new footage.

## What we change to make it OURS (min 3 - never copy a reference 1:1)

1. Colour: swap the blue orb / cyan glitch / purple glow for kit presets (amber->rose gradient keyword, warm coral/amber RGB offsets, neutral card shadows) - zero glow on text.
2. Set: replace the white studio + giant phone with a paper/editorial set (paper texture, taped cards, soft grain) so it is not the same 'white void' look.
3. Transition: keep the push-through-the-phone idea but do it as a paper-tear / card-flip into the screen instead of the stock spin-blur.
4. Text: our own pairing (heavy grotesk + italic serif accent, gradient keyword) instead of the app's default lime caption template.
