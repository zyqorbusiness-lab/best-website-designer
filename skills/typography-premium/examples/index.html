01 // Specimen

Built to beheard, notjust seen.

Bricolage Grotesque at weight 640 for the voice, one Instrument Serif italic keyword, Archivo for reading and JetBrains Mono for labels and numbers. Every size is a clamp() token; the headline tracks at -0.04em and leads at 0.96.

02 // Scale

One scale from 360 to 1440.

03 // Pairing and numbers

Serif for the voice, grotesk for the work.

Body copy sits at 16 to 18 pixels with 1.6 leading and a 62 character measure. Quotes are typographic (“like this”), numbers in tables are tabular so the columns line up, and nothing here is a synthesised bold or italic.
