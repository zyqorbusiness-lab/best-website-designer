# Writing the tier into a client brief

Put this block at the top of every brief, so scope is agreed before any design work.

## NORMAL ($300): "simple but premium"
- Pages: 1 (or up to 3 short pages).
- Signature: one interaction, named here: ______________________.
- 3D: none / one model (name, licence): ______________________.
- Assets: client photos or credited free photos (licence logged); one display + one body font (OFL).
- Budget: own files <= 1.5 MB first load. 0 console errors at 1440 / 390 / 360.
- Delivered: the site, QA screenshots, credits file, how-to-edit note.

## ADVANCED ($1,000): "scroll-based 3D + premium"
- Pages: landing page with 3-5 chapters.
- The scroll moment (one sentence): "As you scroll, ______________________."
- Technique: scroll-scrubbed video / scroll-driven three.js scene / shader / physics (pick one as the star).
- Assets: a real model or real footage (source + licence), our own renders if needed (no AI renders).
- Budget: own files <= 3 MB, 60 fps desktop, >= 45 fps mid phone. Phone uses the same assets, compressed.
- Delivered: + scroll recording, desktop/phone side by side.

## PREMIUM ($10,000): "everything"
- Pages: home + as many inner pages as the content needs; copy written by us.
- Flagship moment: real detailed model, HDRI lighting, 4-6 camera beats, a configurator or exploded view.
- Content: copywriting, photography/model direction, all states (loading, empty, error, success), legal pages.
- Budget: desktop <= 8 MB own files after a finished poster; phone same assets compressed.
- Delivery: fast (dates agreed here: ______), with weekly previews.
- Delivered: + performance numbers, licence file, handover call.

## Never in any tier
Fake reviews or client logos, invented numbers, award promises, AI-generated images presented as real, fonts or
photos without a licence.
