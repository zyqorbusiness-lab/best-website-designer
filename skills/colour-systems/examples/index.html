colour-systems / playground

Three colours, one job each.

Move the seed hue. The page rebuilds its canvas, ink and accent with OKLCH maths and checks every pair against WCAG. Purple hues are moved out of the purple band; a canvas is never black or pure white.

Contrast checks (WCAG 2.2). Goal is the premium target, floor is the legal minimum.
