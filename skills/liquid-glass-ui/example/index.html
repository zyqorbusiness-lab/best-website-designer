The material

Five layers. Drag the glass.

Move the capsule over the fruit and watch the rim bend what is behind it. Switch layers off to see what each one does.

  • RefractionSVG displacement from the shape's signed distance. Chromium.
  • Colour splitRed, green and blue bend by slightly different amounts at the rim.
  • FrostBlur plus saturation, so text stays readable.
  • Specular edgeGlints top-left and bottom-right, a 1px rim that follows the light.
  • DepthInner glow for thickness, a soft coloured shadow under it.

Get it

Three lines, any page.

Copy kit/ into your project. No build step, no dependencies: 15 KB of CSS and 17 KB of JS, about 10 KB gzipped together.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="kit/liquid-glass.css">
<script src="kit/liquid-glass.js" defer></script>
<nav class="lg lg-nav">…</nav>
  • Glass is for controls, not reading. Nav, buttons, sheets, small cards. Long text sits on paper.
  • 4.5:1 for text on glass. Raise --lg-alpha or switch to --dark over busy photos.
  • Budget the blur. 6 to 8 blurred layers on screen on desktop, 3 to 4 on a phone.
  • Every fallback still looks made. No backdrop-filter, reduced transparency, reduced motion, forced colours.
