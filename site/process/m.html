(F) FAQ

Fair questions.

How long does a website take?

A typical landing page takes 1 to 2 weeks. A 3D product site or a scroll-world site takes about 3 to 5 weeks. The real dates depend on the size of the site and how quickly feedback comes back, and they are written into the agreement before work starts.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, what you sell, and the one thing a visitor should do. Your logo, photos or product videos help if you have them. If you do not, I can use licensed stock or build visuals in code.

How much does it cost?

Websites start from $300 for a clean landing page, and go up to around $10k for a full custom 3D or scroll build. You get a fixed price in writing before any work begins.

Will I see progress along the way?

Yes. You approve the brief, the moodboard and the wireframe, and from the build step on you get a private preview link that updates as I work.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Feedback is part of every stage, and it is cheapest early: changing a moodboard takes minutes, changing a finished page takes longer. How many rounds are included is written into the agreement.

Does it work on phones?

Every site ships as two builds: a full desktop version and a lighter phone version that loads fast on mobile data. Phones are sent to the right one automatically.

Are the Lab brands real clients?

No. Stride, Meridian, Volta and the rest are fictional concept brands I designed to show what is possible. They are labelled as concepts on the site.

What tools do you use?

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with three.js for 3D, and GSAP and Lenis for motion and smooth scrolling. Sites are static and fast, and can be hosted on Vercel or your own host.

Can you redesign my current site?

Yes. I start by reviewing what you have, keep what works, and rebuild the rest. My own portfolio went through the same process from version 2 to version 3.
