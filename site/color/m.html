01The wheel

Harmony is geometry.

Colours that work together sit in fixed shapes on the wheel: opposite, side by side, or on the points of a triangle. Drag the white handle to pick a base hue; the shape follows.

HSL: the wheel everyone learns. Fast, but its yellows glow and its blues sink at the same 'lightness'.

Complementary

Two hues straight across the wheel. Maximum tension, so one of them has to be the quiet one.

02Proportion

60 30 10.

The same three colours can look expensive or loud. The difference is how much of each you use: most of the page in the ground, a third in the ink, a sliver of accent.

Three colours, one decision.

03Contrast

Pretty is not readable.

Every pair gets checked against WCAG 2.2 before it ships. Type a hex or use the pickers; the ratio and the pass marks update as you go.

Live preview

Read me at a glance.

Body copy is where weak contrast hurts most: sixteen pixels, long lines, read on a phone in the sun.

Caption, 13px. The smallest thing on the page still has to work.

Our own palette, honestly measured

Ochre at full strength scores 2.88:1 on Ivory: perfect for a 120px numeral, wrong for a caption. Darkened to #8F6420 it reaches 4.49:1, still a hair under the line for body text. So on Ivory, Pine does the reading.
