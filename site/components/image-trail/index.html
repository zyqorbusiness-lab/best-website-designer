Component · image-trail

Image trail

Pictures that follow the pointer and fall away, plus a project list where each row reveals its image beside the cursor. On a phone the list becomes a tap-to-open accordion with the same images.

Vanilla JS2.8 KBTouch accordionPreloadedReduced motion

Move slowly.

Move the pointer or drag a finger here

Hover list · on a phone, tap a row

Use it

Copy the folder image-trail/ (the .js and .css files) into your project. No build step and no framework.

Behaviour

  • Trail images are preloaded once and cloned, so the first pass never flashes empty boxes.
  • Hover list: the floating image eases after the pointer and leans with its speed; the images wipe between rows.
  • Touch and reduced motion: the list becomes an accordion (tap a row to open its image inline, one open at a time); the trail area shows one still image instead of the effect under reduced motion.
  • Rows are real buttons with aria-expanded; floating images are decorative and aria-hidden.

Credits

  • Hover-reveal project lists are a signature of studio portfolios such as Brandon Yasin (Awwwards SOTD Oct 24 2026) and G. Colombel (SOTD Oct 28 2026); image trails are a long-running award-site motif. Written from scratch.
  • Images are Zyqor's own concept renders (models credited on each concept page).
