Component · sticky-story

Sticky story

Chapters scroll while the picture stays pinned; each new chapter wipes its image in, and a rail shows where you are. Built on CSS sticky, so it is smooth on phones too.

Vanilla JS1.6 KBCSS stickyPhone layoutReduced motion

Step 01

One idea

Start with one interaction only this brand could have. For a music school, the ukulele strings are the menu: pluck one to open a page.

Step 02

A real model

No code-made placeholder shapes. Every object is a real scanned or modelled CC0 / CC-BY asset, credited next to the file.

Step 03

Light and motion

HDRI light, a contact shadow and one camera move tied to scroll. The motion explains the product instead of decorating it.

Step 04

Same on the phone

The phone page loads the same model, textures and light as the desktop one. Only the layout changes, never the quality.

01 / 04

Use it

Copy the folder sticky-story/ (the .js and .css files) into your project. No build step and no framework.

Behaviour

  • Pinning is pure CSS position: sticky; JavaScript only decides which step is active (IntersectionObserver at the middle of the screen), so it never jitters.
  • Phone: the media pins under the top bar and the chapters scroll beneath it, with the same images and wipes.
  • Rail buttons have labels and aria-current="step"; inactive frames are aria-hidden.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: frames switch without the wipe and zoom; smooth scrolling is off.

Credits

  • Scroll storytelling as used by Igloo Inc by abeto (Awwwards SOTY 2024) and Persepolis Reimagined by Monks (SOTY 2022). Written from scratch.
  • Images are Zyqor's own concept renders (each model credited on its concept page).
