Component · cursor-follower

Cursor follower

A precise dot and a ring that trails it, grows into a label over work, wraps nav links like a magnet and gets out of the way over text fields. Switches itself off on touch.

Vanilla JS2 KBContext labelsOff on touchReduced motion

On a phone there is no cursor, so this component switches itself off. The tiles keep their own tap and focus states.

Use it

Copy the folder cursor-follower/ (the .js and .css files) into your project. No build step and no framework.

Behaviour

  • Runs only with hover:hover and pointer:fine; on touch it returns null and adds nothing to the page.
  • Over text fields the follower hides and the normal caret shows.
  • The loop stops when the ring has caught up (no idle animation frames).
  • prefers-reduced-motion: no lag, the ring sits exactly on the pointer.
  • Decorative only: aria-hidden, never moves focus.

Credits
