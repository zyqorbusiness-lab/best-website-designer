03 Brew guide

Four steps. Three and a half minutes.

  1. 01Grind

    18 g, medium-fine, like coarse sand.

  2. 02Bloom

    40 g of water at 94°C. Wait 30 seconds.

  3. 03Pour

    Slow circles up to 300 g in total.

  4. 04Rest

    Let it drain to 3:30, then sip slowly.
