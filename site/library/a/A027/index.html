Care

Four habits, a knife for years.

Cut & Grain is a fictional maker; this is how any good knife is looked after.

  1. Wash by hand

    Warm water, a soft sponge, never the dishwasher.

  2. Dry at once

    Wipe from spine to edge, then let the handle air-dry.

  3. Hone weekly

    A few strokes on a ceramic rod keep the edge straight.

  4. Oil the handle

    A drop of food-safe oil once a month.
