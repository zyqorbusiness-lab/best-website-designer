Questions

Plainly answered.

Is this a real company?

No. Tranquility Base is a fictional brand made as a design concept by Zyqor Studio. There are no flights and no prices.

Are the rocks and textures real?

Yes. The boulders are photo-scanned Poly Haven models, the ground is a real regolith texture and the Earth uses NASA imagery. The credits list every file.

Does the phone get a lighter scene?

No. Phone and desktop load the same files and draw the same rocks, stars, terrain and shadows; only the layout changes.
