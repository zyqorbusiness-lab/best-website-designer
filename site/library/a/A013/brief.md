# brief.md: A013 Shelfline Ceramics (Concept)

## Pitch
- One sentence: a ceramics shop as one long shelf; scroll walks the camera past four real vases, each lit as you reach it.
- Matrix card: material glazed ceramic + dark oak / motion slide along a rail / story a tiny museum of one shelf.
- Constraint cards: "No cards" (no product grid; one shelf), "One camera move" (one dolly, dwelling at each vase).
- Category inversion: ceramics shops show a grid of product photos; this is a walk along a lit shelf.
- Distinct from the library: N002 is one vase on a wheel; A013 is a horizontal exhibition of four scans with a moving spotlight and real shadows.

## Frames
- Phone: one vase per screen, camera centred, captions over a paper scrim in the thumb zone.
- Desktop: vase right of centre, caption top left, walk ticks bottom right.
- Same four models, texture, spotlight with 1024 shadow map and HDRI on both.

## States
Scroll walks (dwells at each vase); reduced motion: no easing; no WebGL: poster and captions follow the scroll.

## Three hand-made details
1. Heights measured from the scans (real millimetres) in each caption.
2. The spot slides with you and pools on the wood and wall; vases cast real shadows.
3. Each vase turns a quarter towards you as you reach it; the tick row is the walk.
