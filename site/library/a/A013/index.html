Making

Six steps to the shelf.

The four vases on this page are real 3D scans. Shelfline itself is a fictional shop.

  1. Throw

    Centred and pulled on the wheel.

  2. Dry

    A week under cloth, turned daily.

  3. Bisque

    The first firing.

  4. Glaze

    Dipped or poured, one glaze per piece.

  5. Fire

    A long firing, a slow cool.

  6. Shelf

    It joins the line, lit like the ones above.
