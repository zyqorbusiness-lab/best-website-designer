The oven

Ninety seconds. Nothing to hide.

  1. 485°C

    Oak burns at the back of a clay dome. The floor holds the heat, the flame curls over the top.

  2. 90 s

    One turn on the peel halfway through. The crust blisters, the mozzarella melts but keeps its shape.

  3. 48 h

    The dough rests for two days, so it stays light and tears like bread.
