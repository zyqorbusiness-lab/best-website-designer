01 · The dough 1 / 4

How a Hotcoal pizza comes together

  1. A ball of dough, two days cold.

    Flour, water, salt, yeast. Four things, then a 48-hour rise so it tears like bread.

  2. Opened by hand. Tomato from the middle out.

    Never rolled, so the rim keeps its air. One ladle of crushed tomato, spiralled out to a thumb from the edge.

  3. Fior di latte, torn, not grated.

    Fresh mozzarella in rough pieces, so it melts into pools instead of a sheet.

  4. Then whatever you like on top.

    Pepperoni that cups in the heat, cherry tomatoes, olives, and basil.
