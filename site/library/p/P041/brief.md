# P041 Hotcoal Kitchen (Concept), brief

- Idea: a wood-fired pizzeria whose site IS the order: drag real ingredients onto a real marinara base, fire it, order it.
- Lead reference: a reel by @online.by.annie (Instagram, DfGDN_fIZsC) showing a pizza-builder site branded "Tondo" (tray of bowls, drag onto the base, bake, order card). Used as inspiration only.
- Palette: #F4EDE1 flour paper, #283A2D basil ink (10.4:1), #A93A23 tomato accent (5.4:1 on paper). No black, no purple, no gradients.
- Assets: real photos only (Wikimedia Commons CC0, CC BY 2.0 by Bex Walton, CC BY 3.0 by Dezidor; Poly Haven CC0 texture), cut out by hand-tuned masks.
- v2 (Oct 30, after "real pizza jaisa nahi bana"): canvas renderer; pieces cut from photos of real pizzas at one pixel scale, soft + contact shadows multiplied from one light, raw and baked photo per piece crossfaded on "Fire it" (cheese melts and browns, pepperoni cups, tomatoes blister, basil wilts, crust goes from pale dough to leopard spots), oil/melt pools, steam. See skills/interactive-product-configurator §7b.
- Signature: the pizza is made of the exact pieces you dropped; "Fire it" turns it on the peel while it bakes.
- Desktop: three columns (tray, board 74vh, ticket), drag with a snap ring. Phone (m.html): same pieces, tap to add, sticky total bar.
- Honest: "Concept" labels, form says nothing is sent, credits sheet lists every photo.
