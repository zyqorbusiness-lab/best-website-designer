02 · The dough

It starts as a ball of dough.

Every base is opened by hand on a floured bench, never rolled, so the rim keeps its air. Then it rides the peel into a clay dome with an oak fire at the back.

  1. Flour, water, salt, yeastFour things. Nothing else in it.
  2. 48-hour riseTwo days cold, so it tears like bread.
  3. Stretched by hand, fired in oakOpened from the middle out, then onto the peel.
