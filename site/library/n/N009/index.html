01 · Controls

Every part, one material.

Each part is a real button, input or dialog underneath, so the keyboard and screen readers work before any glass is added.

Segmented control

A glass lens slides to the choice on a spring. Arrow keys move it.

Switch

The thumb turns clear and stretches while you hold it.

Slider

62

A native range on top, a glass lens underneath.

Buttons

Press any of them: they compress, the sheen brightens, then they let go.

Sheet

Escape, a tap outside or Done closes it, and focus comes back here.

Sheet

A sheet made of glass.

It frosts harder than a button because it carries text, and it keeps the room visible at its edges so you never lose your place.

02 · Anatomy

Take one apart.

Every glass surface here is five layers. Switch them off one at a time and watch the tile turn back into a grey box.

  • RefractionThe rim bends the room like a lens.
  • Colour splitRed, green and blue bend by different amounts.
  • FrostBlur and saturation behind a thin tint.
  • Specular edgeA glint top left, a weaker one bottom right.
  • DepthAn inner glow and a soft petrol shadow.

03 · Install

Two tags. No build step.

  1. 01

    Copy the kit folder into your site.

  2. 02

    Add the stylesheet and the script.

  3. 03

    Put lg classes on real buttons, inputs and dialogs.

Safari and Firefox get the frosted glass without the rim refraction. Reduced transparency gets solid surfaces. Reduced motion gets no springs.

index.html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="kit/liquid-glass.css">
<script src="kit/liquid-glass.js" defer></script>

<div class="lg lg-seg" aria-label="Light">
  <button aria-pressed="true">Noon</button>
  <button>Dusk</button>
</div>
<button class="lg-switch" aria-checked="true"
        aria-label="Drift"></button>
