Materials

Three things, nothing else.

  • Walnut base

    Turned from a single block, oiled, never lacquered. The grain on this page is a real walnut veneer scan.

  • Frosted glass orb

    Hand-blown and acid-frosted so the light has no hot spot, only a glow.

  • One warm LED

    A single dimmable source from 1800 K to 3200 K. No blue light before the alarm, by design.
