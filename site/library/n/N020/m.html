How a lesson runs

Fifty minutes, four parts.

Bring nothing but the ukulele. Chordline is a fictional school, so there is no room to visit, but this is how its lessons would run.

  1. Tune

    Five minutes with the clip-on tuner, string by string, low G last.

  2. Warm up

    The chord changes from last week, slowly, until they ring.

  3. New thing

    One new shape or pattern. Never two.

  4. Play together

    The group plays a full song, and someone else takes the melody.
