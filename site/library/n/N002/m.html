How the wheel behaves

Every wheel slows the same way: a little less each turn.

Your drag sets the speed. When you let go, the wheel keeps six percent less of its speed every sixtieth of a second, so a hard flick runs for a few seconds and a nudge stops almost at once. The readout is that number, live, not an animation.

A sideways swipe turns it; an up or down swipe still scrolls the page. With reduced motion on, it never moves unless you move it.
