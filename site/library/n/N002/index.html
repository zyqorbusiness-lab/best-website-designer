How the wheel behaves

Every wheel slows the same way: a little less each turn.

Your drag sets the speed. When you let go, the wheel keeps six percent less of its speed every sixtieth of a second, so a hard flick runs for a few seconds and a nudge stops almost at once. The readout is that number, live, not an animation.

The arrow keys turn it too. With reduced motion on, it never moves unless you move it.

Glazes

Three glazes, fired once each.

Every vase is thrown from the same stoneware body and dipped in one of three glazes. Try them on the wheel above: the swatches change the real model’s glaze.

  • 01 · Natural

    Bone satin

    A soft white that lets the throwing lines show through at the shoulder.

  • 02 · Indigo

    Night indigo

    Pools darker in every groove, so the spiral of the wheel stays visible.

  • 03 · Tomato

    Kitchen tomato

    A warm red that breaks to brown on the rim, where the glaze runs thin.

The studio

Five slow steps, about three weeks.

A small fictional studio with one wheel and one kiln. Visits are on Saturday mornings, by request.

  1. Throw

    Centred and pulled on the wheel in one sitting.

  2. Trim

    Turned leather-hard to thin the foot and sharpen the shoulder.

  3. Bisque

    A first firing, so the clay can take the glaze.

  4. Glaze

    Dipped once, wiped clean at the foot.

  5. Fire

    A long glaze firing and a slow cool, then it is yours.
