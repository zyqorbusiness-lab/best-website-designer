01 · Seasons

Everything changes except the cabin.

TopicWinterSummer
Getting inA path dug from the gate to the porch.A grass track from the pond to the door.
LightShort days. By four the windows are the brightest thing in the woods.Long evenings. It barely gets dark before midnight.
WarmthThe wood stove does the work; the fire pit sleeps under snow.The stove stays cold; evenings end at the fire pit.
The pondFrozen white, ringed by the same stones.Open water, cold enough to wake you up.
Stays the sameThe pines, the stump with the axe in it, and the orange door.

02 · The cabin

Built small on purpose.

One warm room, a sleeping loft under the roof and a porch that faces the pond. Painted pine planks outside, the same green as the trees, and one orange door you can find in a snowstorm.
