A visit

Four steps. No fuss.

  1. Step 01

    Walk in or book

    Walk-ins are always welcome. Booking just holds the chair for you, so there is no wait.

  2. Step 02

    Pick your cut

    Sit down, swing round to the mirror, and tell us what you want. A photo helps.

  3. Step 03

    Hot towel finish

    Every service ends with a hot towel on the neck and a brush-off.

  4. Step 04

    Pay at the chair

    Card or cash, right at the chair. No deposit, no booking fee.
