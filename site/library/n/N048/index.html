A visit

Four turns of the chair.

The chair on this page is a real scanned vintage chair; in the shop it would be the only one.

  1. Sit

    The chair turns to face the mirror. Tell us what you want, or show a photo.

  2. Wash

    A short wash at the basin, warm water, no fragrance unless you ask.

  3. Cut

    Scissors and clippers. We check the back with a hand mirror before we finish.

  4. Finish

    Hot towel on the neck, a brush-off, and the chair turns you to the door.
