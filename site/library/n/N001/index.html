The cloths

01 · Twill

Indigo Denim

A right-hand twill that softens with every wash. We hem jeans with a chain-stitch look and patch the knees that have done their years.

Good for

  • Jeans hems
  • Work jackets
  • Chore coats

02 · Woven check

Glen Check Wool

A check that reads plain from across the room and rich up close. Our house cloth for two-piece suits and a coat that lasts.

Good for

  • Suits
  • Overcoats
  • Trousers

03 · Crepe back satin

Blush Crepe Satin

Matte on one face, liquid on the other. Bridal work lives here: bustles, hems and straps fitted to the shoes you will wear.

Good for

  • Wedding gowns
  • Slip dresses
  • Linings

04 · Quatrefoil jacquard

Fern Jacquard

The quatrefoil is woven into the cloth, not printed on it. Cut for waistcoats and dinner jackets that want one quiet flourish.

Good for

  • Waistcoats
  • Evening jackets
  • Ties

Fittings

How a fitting goes.

Four stops on the tape, from the first measure to the day you collect.

  1. 12 in · Step 1

    Measure

    Twenty minutes and about fifteen measurements, written on your card. Wear the shoes you plan to wear.

  2. 24 in · Step 2

    Choose cloth

    Pick from the rail or bring your own length. We pin a swatch to your card.

  3. 36 in · Step 3

    First fitting

    A basted try-on two weeks later. Chalk, pins and small changes while you stand.

  4. 48 in · Step 4

    Collect

    Pressed, bagged and checked once more on you. Small tweaks in the first month are on us.
