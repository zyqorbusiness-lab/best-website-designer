01 · The cloths

Four lengths, chosen by hand.

Every cloth is a real scanned textile with its own weave, sheen and weight of light. Hang any of them on the rod above.

  1. 01

    Indigo Denim

    Twill

    Cotton twill, indigo warp over an undyed weft. Breaks in to its owner over a year of wear.

    Cut forJackets, chore coats, trousers

  2. 02

    Glen Check Wool

    Woven check

    A small glen check in worsted wool with a faint rose overcheck. Reads plain across a room, patterned up close.

    Cut forSuits, overcoats, waistcoats

  3. 03

    Blush Crepe Satin

    Crepe back satin

    Liquid on the face, matte crepe on the back. Catches light along the thread, so it changes as you move.

    Cut forLinings, evening shirts, slip dresses

  4. 04

    Fern Jacquard

    Quatrefoil jacquard

    The quatrefoil is woven in, not printed: a shine-and-matte figure that only shows when light rakes across it.

    Cut forWaistcoats, evening jackets, ties

02 · How a fitting goes

Four steps, one garment.

  1. Step 01

    Choose the cloth

    Hang each length, hold it to the window, take a swatch home if you want to sleep on it.

  2. Step 02

    Measure by hand

    Your cutter takes your measurements and notes how you stand, not only how long your arms are.

  3. Step 03

    Basted try-on

    The garment is tacked together in white thread so the shape can still move before anything is final.

  4. Step 04

    Final fitting

    Last pins at the hem and cuff, then the finished piece is pressed and ready to collect.
