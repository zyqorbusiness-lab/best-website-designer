# N001 Loomhouse Tailors: brief (best-4 build #3, Oct 31 2026)

Fictional tailor and alterations shop, labelled "Concept" in the nav and footer. Built to docs/beast/specs/loomhouse-tailors_spec.md (section 10 lists what changed).

- Beats: tailor's tape preloader (blade extends with real load progress) -> #hero cloth on a lacquered rod; four pinked swatches flood the cloth from the exact spot you point at and re-theme the page from the cursor -> #cloths: four pinned chapters (denim, glen check, crepe satin, jacquard), each floods the cloth and pushes the camera into the weave -> #alterations: drag the chalk line on a glen check trouser leg (1 px = 1/16 in, snaps to 1/4 in), Hem / Take in / Let out / Zip, live ticket with sample price -> #fitting: a tape that scrolls sideways, steps at 12/24/36/48 in -> #book (Concept: not sent) + visit details -> footer.
- Files: index.html (desktop), m.html (phone), style.css, m.css, app.js (scroll engine, UI), cloth.js (three.js cloth + flood), tape.js (preloader), tex/ (Poly Haven CC0 scans), poster.webp, thumb.webp, credits.json, credits.md, qa.json.
- Fonts: Instrument Serif, Inter Tight, JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL 1.1, self-hosted). Palette: rose #F6E2E5, moss #293E20, fern #3B8C5F plus tints; flat fills only.
- Phone = desktop: same mesh density, same textures, same HDRI, same renderer settings (qa-library: same 3D files, same effects).
