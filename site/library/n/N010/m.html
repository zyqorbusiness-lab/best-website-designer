Root to crate

Four steps, nothing added.

  1. 01

    Picked by hand

    We pick to colour and scent, never to a date on a calendar.

  2. 02

    Rested in shade

    A day in the packhouse so the peel firms up before it travels.

  3. 03

    Wrapped in paper

    Each fruit in its own sheet. No wax, no plastic tray.

  4. 04

    At your door

    Out the next morning in a vented wooden crate.
