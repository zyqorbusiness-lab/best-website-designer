# Credits: Rootline Citrus (Concept N010)

Rootline Citrus is a fictional brand made by Zyqor Studio as a web concept. Not a real company, not a client, nothing for sale. Every third-party asset below is used under its licence; each asset file also has a `<file>.credit.json` next to it.

Oct 31 2026 refresh: the 3D scans were replaced by real fruit photography (whole and cut open) so the fruit looks like food; the colour flood, switcher, copy and crate ledger are unchanged.

Oct 31 2026 round 2 (3D rule): the fruit is 3D again. citrus.js builds a whole fruit and a cut half in three.js: shaped sphere, oil-gland normal map and mottling drawn in code, and a custom cut-face shader (segments, membranes, pith ring, radial juice sacs with wet highlights and a back-lit translucent glow). The scroll camera flies the half from the hero into the colour disc and orbits round its side. The real photos stay as cards beside the 3D fruit.
