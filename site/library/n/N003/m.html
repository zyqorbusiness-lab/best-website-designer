01 · The flake

Hollow, thin, loud.

Each flake grows as an upside-down pyramid floating on the brine. That hollow shape is why it crunches, and why a pinch looks generous on the plate.

  • 01 · Crunch

    A pyramid flake shatters between the teeth instead of dissolving straight away.

  • 02 · Finish

    Scatter it last, on tomatoes, on chocolate, on a fried egg.

  • 03 · Pinch

    Wide flakes are easy to see, so you salt less and taste more.

03 · Harvest

Sun, wind, a rake.

  1. 01

    Let the tide in

    Sea water is let into shallow clay beds on a spring tide and left to the sun and wind.

  2. 02

    Wait for the crust

    As the brine warms, hollow pyramid crystals grow on the surface and float there.

  3. 03

    Rake it by hand

    The flakes are lifted off the surface before they sink, then dried in moving air.
