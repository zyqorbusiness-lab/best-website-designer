## Shared look
- **Framed talking head** (01, 09, 16): the speaker sits inside a rounded vertical card (~60 % frame width, radius ~8 %), white/cream page #f8f8f8 with an orange #f5a000-ish corner blob, small "Before/After" chips, handle at the bottom; a second smaller card for the "before". Warm skin/brown tones #553028 / #d3ad7e. 0 hard cuts in 17–19 s — the card stays, captions and stickers change. Onsets 2.1–2.8 /s.
- **Hormozi / billion-dollar style** (05): desaturated grey world #6e6c6c / #3c3738 / #999898, one accent (red, money green), lowercase rounded sans "the billion dollar man", cut-out photos; cuts land on **luma flashes** (−78…+163/255 at 6.13, 11.70, 14.93, 17.47 s); blurred-in text (blur runs 11–25 frames = text arriving out of a blur).
- **Purple motion-design promo** (13): white #ffffff + violet #6d28d9-ish fills, serif-italic display ("Motion Design") mixed with bold sans, 3D props (eyeball, brain, TV) as stickers, 9 cuts in 18 s, onsets 3.4 /s (gap 0.23 s), flashes on cuts.
- **Dark cinematic fan edit** (03): teal-black #1e272b / #030b0e, 14 cuts in 20 s after a 9.8 s slow intro (shots 0.2–3.3 s), flicker clusters (1–2 frame luma jumps) before the drop at 8.0–8.3 s.
- Overlays: light-leak bursts on black (14: bursts +49…+92 luma at 4.90/5.53/6.20 and 10.90/11.47 → pairs ~0.6 s apart, screen-blend them), embers/sparks on black (04, 15), film dust/scratches (07).

## Do this (rules)
1. Talking head in a rounded card: card 60–65 % of width, 8 % corner radius, cream bg, one brand-colour blob in a corner, handle at the bottom; keep the card on screen the whole reel, animate captions/chips instead of cutting. — 01 https://www.pinterest.com/pin/671599363236283537/ , 09 https://www.pinterest.com/pin/671599363236245110/
2. Before/After split: small "before" card (~35 % width) tucked behind the big "after" card, label chips with a dot. — 01, 16 https://www.pinterest.com/pin/654640495884010181/
3. Text arrives out of blur: 10–20 frames from gaussian blur ~20 px + 110 % scale to sharp 100 %, ease-out. — 05 https://www.pinterest.com/pin/1147643917677607792/
4. Cut on a luma flash: 1–2 frame white (+90…+160 luma) or black dip on the cut frame, on the music onset. — 05, 13 https://www.pinterest.com/pin/924363892282992611/
5. Slow intro then drop: ~8–10 s of long shots, a 3–6 frame flicker cluster, then 0.3–1.5 s shots on every beat. — 03 https://www.pinterest.com/pin/1023794927798348621/
6. Light-leak overlay in screen mode on transitions, 15–20 frames per burst, warm red→orange (#ff3b00 → #ffb000). — 14 https://www.pinterest.com/pin/603200943843150335/
7. Embers/sparks or film-dust overlay at 20–40 % opacity over dark cinematic edits for texture. — 04 https://www.pinterest.com/pin/1090715603482767751/ , 07 https://www.pinterest.com/pin/1030057745993566220/
8. One display font pairing per reel: bold sans for keywords + an italic serif accent word, 2–4 words per caption. — 13, 08 https://www.pinterest.com/pin/30117891252303371/
