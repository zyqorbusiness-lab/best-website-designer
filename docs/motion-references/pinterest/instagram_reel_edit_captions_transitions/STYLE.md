## The shared look
- **Captions:** 1–3 words at a time, centred in the upper-middle or right under the face, never bottom-of-screen subtitles. Big bold white sans with a soft shadow (01 "you", 16 "you"), or the "modern real estate" style: bold sans + ONE word in italic serif or a bright colour (04, 10 "Before / After" label, orange/yellow highlights). Lowercase, small, elegant serif labels for aesthetic montages (02 "the details.", 03 "the outfit.").
- **Title cards:** a script/handwritten title over the clip for tutorials ("How To Do This" — 06, 14), or a stacked serif quote in yellow/white (08).
- **Framing:** talking head shrunk into a rounded card on a clean background with blurred brand shapes (10, Motion_pet), split screens 2–3 panels (02, 03, 05, 07 "LIFE IN JUNE" panel reveal).
- **Transitions:** match cut / object-wipe (09 coffee transition), speed ramp with motion blur (14, 06), mask reveal through text or panels (07), zoom punch-in on the key word; hard cuts land on the beat.
- **Colour/grade:** warm, soft, slightly faded film look for lifestyle; clean bright white/neutral for talking-head business (04, 10).
- **Pacing:** hook text on frame 1 ("Want captions that actually pop?" 01; "How To Do This" 06/14); a caption change every 0.3–0.6 s; a visual change (cut, zoom, card, b-roll) at least every 2 s.
- **CTA:** "Comment 'Intro' to get the template" in the caption/description (06), "save this", follow — keyword-comment CTA is the standard.

## Do this (rules an editor can copy)
1. **Hook in frame 1 = text on screen**, a question or "How to …" in large type, before anyone speaks. → 01, 06, 14
2. **Captions 1–3 words, centre-upper or chest height, big (90–130 px at 1080 w), bold white sans with soft shadow**; pop in with a 1.0→1.1→1.0 scale. → 01, 16
3. **One emphasis word per line**: switch it to italic serif or a brand colour (yellow/orange/green), never more than one. → 04, 10 (Motion_pet "real estate style subtitles")
4. **Talking head in a rounded card** (radius ~40 px) over a soft brand background or a blurred copy of the clip; card slides/scales between sections. → 10, 04
5. **Zoom punch-in (1.0→1.15 in 0.15 s) on the key word** instead of a cut; hold, then ease back. Talking-head reels here have 0–3 hard cuts. → 04, 05
6. **Transitions that hide the cut**: object/hand wipe, whip with motion blur, speed ramp (fast 0.2 s, slow 0.6 s). Put a whoosh on every one. → 09, 13, 14, 06
7. **Script title + tutorial layout** for how-to reels: "How To Do This" handwritten at top, steps as 1-word labels. → 06, 14
8. **Split-screen / panel montage for b-roll**: 2–3 stacked panels, small lowercase serif labels. → 02, 03, 07
9. **Mix at −14 LUFS**, music bed under voice; never ship a quiet −22 LUFS voice (01).
10. **CTA = keyword comment** ("Comment SITE and I'll send it") on screen in the last 2 s and in the caption. → 06
