# STYLE — "saas product promo motion graphics" (Pinterest, 2026-11-03)

Source: Pinterest video search, logged out (public), 16 pins, 8 downloaded (vids/01–08, 720p, ≤30 s). Look at `sheet.jpg` (all 16) and `frames_sheet.jpg` (6 frames each for 01–08).
Measured on 01–08: avg shot 0.5–3.4 s (01 Moolah = 29 cuts in 15.8 s; 04 Glass / 05 Frames / 06 Budget ≈ 1.8–2.4 s); 02 and 08 have NO hard cuts (one continuous move). Audio: music-only beds at −13 to −15 LUFS (07 at −10), no voice-over in any of them. 6 of 8 are 9:16 at 720x1280; 03 is 16:9 letterboxed (avoid).

## The shared look
- **Layout:** product UI is the hero, never a flat screenshot — phone mockups floating at an angle (01), account/feature rows as floating cards (13), UI cards fanned or stacked in 3D (01 "Slow & Steady / Balanced / Thrill Seeker" cards, 16 curved dashboard ring, 07 card carousel arc), dashboards on a tilted plane with depth (09, 12, 10). Lots of empty space around one object.
- **Typography:** clean geometric/grotesk sans (Inter / General Sans / Satoshi type), 2–4 words per card, very large ("Invest effortlessly", "Tailored to you", "AMAZING"), tight leading, left- or top-aligned. Accent = one word in a second style: colour (05 red glowing "audience"), italic (07 "Who dream big"), or script (05 "Instagram").
- **Colour:** ONE bold brand colour per video filling the whole frame: violet/indigo (02, 03, 07, 16), cobalt blue on white/grey (04, 09, 12), flat pink → yellow → red full-frame swaps (01), or dark UI with one neon accent (08 orange, 14 green, 15 green). Soft gradients and glass/frosted cards (04, 06), never busy textures.
- **Motion:** every beat is a move, not a cut — scale-up reveals, cards sliding in on springy ease-out, 3D rotate/tilt of the screen, camera dolly into a UI detail, mask/wipe reveals of words (03 "Every brand starts", "BRANDS" wipe), colour-field swaps on the beat (01). Continuous scroll of a landing page with slow push-in (08 — the "website as product" format, fits Zyqor's concept sites exactly).
- **Pacing:** a new visual beat every ~0.5–1 s in the fast ones (01, 05), 2 s in the calm premium ones (04, 06). Hook = the product or a bold 2-word claim already on screen in frame 1 (01 phone, 05 "You've got the ideas and content").
- **Sound:** upbeat/minimal electronic bed, whoosh/pop on each card entrance, music only (no VO) — text carries the story.
- **CTA / end:** logo lockup on a solid brand colour (01 Moolah pig on red, 04 "BLUE BOX™", 02 "DROP POINT"), or a pill button ("Join us" with arrow, 07). 1–1.5 s hold.

## Do this (rules an editor can copy)
1. **Never show a flat screen.** Put every screenshot on a floating card/phone with perspective tilt (rotateX 8–15°, rotateY −10–15°), soft shadow, and slowly drift/rotate it the whole time it is on screen. → 01, 09, 12, 16
2. **One brand colour fills the frame; swap the whole background on the beat** for a chapter change (pink → yellow → red). → 01, 07, 02
3. **2–4 word headlines, huge, sans, one accent word** (different colour, italic or glow). Max one headline on screen at a time. → 01 "Invest effortlessly", 05 "audience" in red glow, 07 "Who dream big"
4. **Fan or stack the features as cards** that slide in one by one (stagger 0.12–0.2 s, ease-out back). Each card = icon + 2-word label + one number. → 01 risk cards, 13 account rows, 06 floating chips
5. **Move the camera into the UI** where the "click" happens: push-in 1.0→1.35 scale on the focused element, the rest blurs slightly. → 10, 12 (horizontal, but the move is the lesson)
6. **Scroll the real website as the product**: dark page, slow continuous scroll with a gentle zoom, section by section, no hard cuts (perfect for Zyqor concept sites). → 08 (Redsun template)
7. **Beat every 0.5–1 s for social, 2 s for premium**; cut or move on the music's beat; pop/whoosh sfx on every entrance, mixed at −14 LUFS, no voice needed. → 01 (0.53 s/shot), 04 (2.4 s/shot)
8. **Glass + soft 3D objects for a premium feel**: frosted cards, translucent blue cubes/folders, gentle light sweep. → 04, 06, 16
9. **End on a lockup on solid colour**: logo + one-line tagline, or a pill button "Join us →" / "DM me". Hold 1–1.5 s. → 01, 04, 07
10. **Avoid**: 16:9 content letterboxed into 9:16 (03, 10, 12, 14 are horizontal and waste the screen), fake stats — use real or clearly-concept numbers.
